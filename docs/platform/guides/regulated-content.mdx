Medical, legal, and financial copy has a higher bar: a changed dosage, a softened claim, or a wrong defined term is a real problem, not a style issue. This guide sets up a project so AI translates, checks catch what matters, and people review everything the rules say they must.

You configure every part of this yourself, per project. You need the admin or localization manager role.

## 1. Mark the project's content domain

On the project's **QA** page, open **Check settings** and set **Content domain** to **Medical**, **Legal**, or **Financial**. The domain:

- Tells the quality estimator which changes are severe, such as a dosage, a defined term, or an amount.
- Raises the lowest estimate threshold to 85 for medical and legal, and to 80 for financial.
- Makes every **Run QA** workflow step report that the work needs human review, whatever the score.

See [Content domain](/platform/help/quality#content-domain).

## 2. Write down your rules

Quality checks can only enforce what you write down.

| Rule | Where it goes |
| --- | --- |
| Approved and banned terms, such as drug names, claim wording, and regulated phrases | A [glossary](/platform/knowledge). Mark banned alternatives as forbidden. |
| Tone, claims you may not make, required disclaimers, and the regulator's style rules | A [guideline document](/platform/knowledge/guidelines). Mark critical passages **Always apply**. |
| Previously approved wording | A [translation memory](/platform/translation-memory-import-export). Import your existing approved translations. |
| Product names and terms a spellchecker should accept | A spellcheck dictionary in **Knowledge**. |

## 3. Turn on the checks

In **Check settings**:

1. Turn on **Guidelines** and set it to **Error**. Each scan checks translations against your guideline documents, with AI acting as the judge, and cites the passage that was broken.
2. Turn on **Estimated quality**. AI scores each segment from 0 to 100 for how ready it is to publish without edits. Segments below the threshold become findings.
3. Turn on **Quality score** and set the **Pass threshold**. Each finding costs points by severity, and a language passes only when it reaches the threshold and has no critical finding. Raise the penalty for checks that matter most to you.
4. Keep the placeholder, glossary, and length checks on.
5. Turn on the daily scan, or run a scan after each batch.

See [Project QA](/platform/help/quality#project-qa) for how each check and the score work.

## 4. Send work to people

Create a workflow from the **AI translation with a QA gate** template under **Automations → New**. It translates each new source file with AI, runs QA, and then:

- Announces work that needs no review as ready to ship.
- Assigns a post-edit review task per language for everything else, due in 48 hours.

Because the content domain is set, every run takes the review path. QA and the estimate still tell the reviewer which segments to look at first.

To add a formal sign-off, such as a medical or legal reviewer approving a release, add a **Human approval** step after the review tasks. The run waits until the named person or team chooses **Approve**, **Reject**, or **Request changes** in **Inbox**. See [Pause for a reviewer](/platform/visual-workflows#pause-for-a-reviewer).

## 5. Limit who can approve

Give translators and agencies the translator role, so they can edit but not approve. Give reviewers the reviewer role, and only for the projects they own. See [Roles and permissions](/platform/workspace/roles).

## 6. Track problems to the end

On the **QA** page, select findings and choose **Create issues** to track them on the project's **Board**, or **Send to Linear** to file them in your team's Linear. Ignoring a finding asks for a reason, which is kept with the finding.

## What this does not cover

- Checks run on text. Review images and video by eye.
- The domain presets are fixed. Use guidelines and glossary terms for rules specific to your content.
- QA findings support a reviewer. They do not replace one. Keep a qualified reviewer on regulated content.
