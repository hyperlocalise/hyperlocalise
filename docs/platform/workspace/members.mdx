---
title: "Invite teammates and manage access"
description: "Invite workspace members, assign roles, and use teams to give access to projects."
---

Invite teammates from **Members** in the **Workspace** sidebar. Choose a role for what they can do and a team for the project access they need.

## Send an invitation

1. Open **Members** and select **Invite member**.
2. Enter the teammate’s **Email**.
3. Choose a **Role** and **Team**.
4. Select **Send invitation**.
5. Ask the teammate to accept the invitation and sign in with the invited email address.

A pending invitation is not an active membership. The member becomes active after accepting and signing in. If the workspace has no team, the invitation flow can create a default team.

Someone who already belongs to another workspace can also accept from the workspace menu: open **View all workspaces** and select **Accept** next to the invitation.

## Create another workspace

Anyone signed in can create a separate workspace from the workspace menu with **Create workspace**. Each workspace has its own projects, members, settings and plan; you stay a member of your other workspaces and switch between them from the same menu.

## Change access

Use **Change role…** and **Update role** to change a member’s workspace role. Open the team to add existing workspace members with **Add team member**. A person must be a workspace member before you can add them to a team.

Review [Roles and permissions](/platform/workspace/roles) before giving approval or administration access. Do not solve a missing-project problem by granting a broader workspace role without checking team access first.

## Revoke or remove

Use **Revoke invitation…** for an invitation that should no longer be accepted. Use **Remove member…** when an existing teammate should lose workspace access. Personal tokens tied to a removed membership stop working; inspect automations or tools that depended on that user’s connection.

## Leave a workspace

Open the workspace menu and select **Leave workspace…**. The dialog lists your API keys, which stop working, and the automations that use accounts you connected, which may stop working. Select **Leave workspace** to confirm; you then move to another of your workspaces. To come back, an admin has to invite you again.

The only admin cannot leave. Make another member an admin first; an invitation that has not been accepted does not count.

If a teammate cannot find the workspace or project, follow [Access troubleshooting](/platform/help/access).
