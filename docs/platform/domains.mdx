Use **Domains** to connect keyword research and ranking checks to a website you control. Research is scoped to a hostname and market, so you can compare the wording people search for in each region.

## Start with your website

1. [Link and verify your domain](/platform/research/verify-domain).
2. Choose the markets relevant to your content.
3. [Research keywords and track rankings](/platform/research/keywords-and-ranks).

If **Domains** is not available in your workspace, ask your administrator or [support](/platform/help/contact-support) about access.

## Choose a market

Open the domain and use the locale selector in the page header. The selected market applies as you move between **Overview**, **Keyword research**, and **Rank tracking**. An empty market view means no data is saved for that market; it does not substitute another market’s metrics.

Use the markets offered in the selector. A research market combines language and location and is separate from a translation project’s target locales.

## Use research in localization

Research the local search intent before changing translated titles or headings. Save promising keywords, inspect their search results, and add approved terminology or market context to the project’s [language resources](/platform/knowledge).

Research does not automatically rewrite or publish your website. Review and deliver the content through your normal translation and publishing workflow.
