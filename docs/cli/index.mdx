Bring Hyperlocalise Platform into your development workflow. Use Hyperlocalise CLI
to upload source files from your repository and pull translations back into your
app. Your team manages translation, review, and language resources in one shared
workspace, while you keep delivery in Git and CI.

## Start with Hyperlocalise Platform

For teams, we recommend pairing the CLI with Hyperlocalise Platform. Developers
keep working with files and pull requests; translators and managers work in the
same project without needing a local CLI setup.

## From source files to your next release

1. [Create a project](/platform/projects/create-project) and choose your target languages.
2. [Install the CLI](/cli/getting-started/install) and [connect your project](/platform/cli).
3. Preview your upload with `hyperlocalise sync push --dry-run`, then upload with `hyperlocalise sync push`.
4. [Start a translation](/platform/translation/start) and [review it with your team](/platform/cat) in Platform.
5. Preview the download with `hyperlocalise sync pull --dry-run`, then retrieve translations with `hyperlocalise sync pull`.
6. Review the file changes and deliver them through your existing release process.

Source uploads and translation runs are separate steps. Set up a
[Source upload automation](/platform/automations) when you want uploads to trigger
translation. Pull retrieves project translations; review their readiness before release.
